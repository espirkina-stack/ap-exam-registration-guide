Registering for AP exams

CGA prepares students for AP exams. AP Exams are administered by College Board.

  • The College Board does not allow online schools like CGA to administer or register students for AP exams.
  • All CGA students must register directly with an approved AP exam center.
  • The list below shows schools where CGA students have previously registered and which may accept private candidates.
  • If there is no school near you, use the Course Ledger and Google to find another option and contact the school directly.

About the list

We put this list together to make your first steps easier. It includes schools where CGA students have registered in the past, along with the contact details and links we have available.

Details can change, so please always check directly with the school before making plans. If a school on the list is no longer accepting private candidates, or if you spot information that needs updating, we would be grateful if you let us know.

This list is here to help — not to limit your options

These are centres our students have used before, but they are only a starting point. There are many other schools worldwide that may accept private candidates. If you do not see your country or city, please use the College Board Course Ledger, ask classmates and search online for a nearby school.

Deadlines

The official College Board registration deadline is November 13, 2026. Private-candidate registration often closes earlier, so aim to register before October 2.

Top tips

  1. When reaching out to schools, use the contact template.
  2. Tell schools that you are home-schooled rather than studying at an online school.
  3. You are registered only after you have paid and joined the exam section on the College Board website.
  4. If asked for CGA’s 6-digit College Board school code, use 703001.
